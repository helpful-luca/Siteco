# Arenis LED floodlight

Nordlicht Leuchten GmbH (fictional), product datasheet Arenis, issue 03/2026

Arenis is a high-output LED floodlight for sports grounds, logistics yards, building facades and large car parks. The die-cast aluminium housing has cooling fins on the back, and each optic sits behind a flat tempered glass cover. A single housing size covers 100 W to 290 W.

## Applications

Sports lighting planned to EN 12193, for example football training pitches and tennis courts
Outdoor work places planned to EN 12464-2, for example container yards and loading areas
Facade and object lighting with the narrow beam optics

## Photometry

Beam options: narrow N20 (20°), medium M40 (40°), wide W60 (60°), asymmetric A45 (peak at 45°)
Colour temperatures: 3000 K, 4000 K, 5000 K
Colour rendering: CRI Ra 70 or CRI Ra 80, depending on the variant
ULOR: 0 % at 0° tilt with the asymmetric A45 optic
Photobiological safety: risk group RG1 according to EN 62471
Lumen maintenance: L90B10 60 000 h and L80B10 100 000 h, both at Ta 25 °C

## Variants

Values for system power including driver, tolerance ±10 %.
NL-7230-3KW60: 3000 K, CRI Ra 80, wide W60, 12 500 lm, 100 W, 125 lm/W
NL-7230-4KN20: 4000 K, CRI Ra 70, narrow N20, 21 500 lm, 150 W, 143 lm/W
NL-7230-4KM40: 4000 K, CRI Ra 70, medium M40, 21 000 lm, 150 W, 140 lm/W
NL-7230-4KA45: 4000 K, CRI Ra 70, asymmetric A45, 30 500 lm, 220 W, 139 lm/W
NL-7230-5KA45: 5000 K, CRI Ra 80, asymmetric A45, 38 000 lm, 290 W, 131 lm/W
The variant with the highest output, NL-7230-5KA45, is intended for training pitches and large yards.

# Arenis LED floodlight: technical data

## Electrical

Supply voltage: 220 V to 240 V AC, 50/60 Hz
Protection class: I
Power factor: above 0.95 at full load
Surge protection: 6 kV (common and differential mode)
Driver: DALI-2, optional 1-10 V (order suffix -V)
Control interface: Zhaga Book 18 receptacle on the driver box for a daylight sensor or a wireless node
Inrush current: 45 A for 250 microseconds per luminaire

## Mechanical

Housing: die-cast aluminium, powder coated, graphite grey
Cover: tempered glass, 5 mm
Ingress protection: IP66
Impact resistance: IK10
Dimensions (L x W x H): 480 x 410 x 95 mm
Weight: 11.2 kg
Wind area: 0.19 m² (front, at 0° tilt)
Mounting: hot-dip galvanised steel U-bracket with a centre hole of Ø 18 mm and two slots for M12 bolts
Tilt range: -90° to +90° with a scale in 5° steps
Mounting height: 6 m to 30 m

## Environment

Operating temperature: -30 °C to +45 °C
Storage temperature: -40 °C to +60 °C
Vibration: tested for mounting on masts up to 30 m

## Standards, warranty and accessories

Safety: EN 60598-1 and EN 60598-2-5 (floodlights)
EMC: EN 55015, EN 61547, EN 61000-3-2
Marks: CE, ENEC
Warranty: 5 years on luminaire and driver
NL-7299-SH01: glare visor, reduces spill light above the horizontal plane
NL-7299-LV02: louvre for the narrow and medium optics
NL-7299-AB30: aiming device for installation at night
